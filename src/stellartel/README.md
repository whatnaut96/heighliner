# StellarTel

StellarTel is a basic data engineering project for Elite Dangerous.
Journals are received from `beacon` on the game's machine and sent here for processing.

## Journal Types Supported
### FSDJump
FSDJump events are used to get the information for systems, their coordinates, and their status.

### Docked
Docked events are used to get the information for stations within systems, their landing pad count, their individual factions and statuses, and other related data.

### Market
Market evens are used for maintaining a list of commodities at a particular station and they economic information.
